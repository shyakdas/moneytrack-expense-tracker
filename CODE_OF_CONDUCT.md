# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a
**harassment-free experience for everyone**, regardless of age,
gender, identity, experience, or background.

---

## Our Standards

Examples of positive behavior include:
- Being respectful and inclusive
- Accepting constructive feedback
- Showing empathy toward others

Unacceptable behavior includes:
- Harassment or discrimination
- Personal attacks or insults
- Trolling or disruptive behavior
- Publishing private information without consent

---

## Our Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for:
- Clarifying standards of acceptable behavior
- Taking appropriate corrective action
- Ensuring a safe community environment

---

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the maintainer.
All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within project spaces and in public spaces
when representing the project.
